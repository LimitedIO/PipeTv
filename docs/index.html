CH 01
CH 01 · HOME

Opens instantly. Your feed is already there.

The last feed is cached, so there is something to watch before the network even answers. Recommendations grow from what you watch, mixed with trending, with no account behind them.

  • Virtualised grid with spring physics
  • Thumbnails decoded at card size
  • A calm, slowly drifting background
CH 02
CH 02 · NAVIGATION

Every page, one press away.

Press ← from the first column and the menu slides in: Home, Search, Popular, Favorites, History, Settings. No hunting, no nested menus.

CH 03
CH 03 · POPULAR

Channel surfing, the way it should be.

Flip between topics like changing channels. Each one loads in place, no page reloads.

  • Trending · Music · Gaming · News · Sports
  • Movies · Learning · Comedy · Live
CH 04
CH 04 · SEARCH

A keyboard built for the remote.

Big, color-coded keys with action rows on top. Suggestions update with every letter, and recent searches are one press away.

  • 14 layouts: English, العربية, Русский, हिन्दी…
  • Voice search from the remote's mic button
  • Any physical keyboard types straight in
CH 05
CH 05 · FILTERS

Videos, channels or playlists. Instantly.

Switch filters without searching again. Results keep loading as you scroll, so there's no "next page" button.

CH 06
CH 06 · PLAYBACK

Press OK. It just plays.

A clear "Opening…" with the thumbnail while it loads, and the current video keeps playing until the new one is ready.

  • ← → seek 10 → 30 → 60 s when held
  • 0–9 jump to 0–90 %
  • Retries by itself if a stream hiccups
CH 07
CH 07 · PLAYER OPTIONS

Quality, speed, subtitles, audio.

Press ↑ while watching. Big sliders and pop-up menus that feel like an old TV's on-screen display: pick resolution, speed, any subtitle track, or the dub and audio language.

CH 08
CH 08 · DETAILS

Read the comments without stopping the show.

Press ↓ while it plays: description, facts, comments with replies, up next and a jump to the channel. The video keeps playing.

CH 09
CH 09 · MINI PLAYER

Keep browsing. It keeps playing.

Press Back and the video shrinks into a live preview in the menu. Press ← again for the larger player panel, or select it to go back to fullscreen.

CH 10
CH 10 · QUICK ACTIONS

Hold OK. Do anything.

Hold OK on any card to play, open info, favorite, or jump to the uploader's channel. The red or bookmark key on your remote favorites in one press.

  • Favorites for videos, channels and playlists
  • History by day, with resume points
CH 11
CH 11 · SETTINGS

SponsorBlock built in. Settings that stay simple.

Sponsor segments are skipped silently and marked on the progress bar. Every option opens its own focused pop-up, so nothing gets lost.

  • 10 languages with full right-to-left layout
  • Content region separate from app language
  • Updates itself from GitHub Releases

FAQ

Questions

Is PipeTv really free, with no ads?

Yes. It's open source under GPL-3.0, has no ads and asks for no account. Sponsor segments inside videos are skipped with SponsorBlock.

How can I watch YouTube without ads on Android TV or Fire TV?

Install PipeTv: download the APK from GitHub Releases and sideload it with the Downloader app, a USB drive or adb. It plays YouTube without ads and without signing in.

Is it a NewPipe for Android TV?

It uses NewPipe's extractor under the hood, with an interface designed from scratch for TVs and remotes.

Is PipeTv an alternative to SmartTube?

Yes, PipeTv is another free, open-source way to watch YouTube on a TV. It focuses on a simple, calm interface, no account at all and a keyboard built for the remote.

Does it work on Fire TV, Google TV or an old Android box?

Anything running Android 5.0 or newer: Android TV, Google TV, Chromecast with Google TV, Fire TV sticks and generic boxes.

Do I need a Google account?

No. PipeTv uses no Google services. Favorites and history are stored on your TV.

How do updates work?

PipeTv checks GitHub Releases on launch, downloads new versions in the background and offers to install them. A daily job ships a new build whenever NewPipeExtractor updates.
