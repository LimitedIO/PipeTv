YouTube on your TV, like it used to feel. A fast, private, remote-first YouTube client for Android TV, Google TV and Fire TV.

• No ads, no account, no Google services
• SponsorBlock built in
• Instant home feed, cached so it shows before the network answers
• Custom on-screen keyboard (14 layouts) with live suggestions and voice search
• Search filters for videos, channels and playlists
• Details, comments with replies and up-next while a video plays
• Watch history timeline with resume points
• Quality, speed and subtitle controls with sliders and steppers
• 10 languages with full right-to-left support
• Android 5.0+
